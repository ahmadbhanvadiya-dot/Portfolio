# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Recruiters, hackathon teammates, collaborators and fellow developers who arrive from a GitHub profile, LinkedIn or a résumé link. They want to know, in under a minute, who Ahmad is, what he has actually built, and how to reach him.

## Product Purpose

A single-page personal portfolio for Ahmad Bhanvadiya, a Computer Science student and developer. Success: a visitor opens a project or sends an email, and leaves with the impression that the work is careful and the author has taste.

## Positioning

Real, verifiable projects (each links to its public repository, and to a live demo where one exists) presented with restraint. No invented metrics, achievements or testimonials.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Next.js 16 (App Router), React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS v4. Deployed on Vercel.
- Sections: Navigation, Hero, About, Selected Work, Skills, Contact, Footer.
- Status line: open to internships and collaborations (confirmed).
- Résumé at `public/Resume.pdf` stays linked (confirmed).
- FlightFare is excluded: no verifiable repository (confirmed).

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Ahmad Bhanvadiya. Monogram "AB".
- Binding visual brief from the owner: minimal, simple, elegant, intentional. Warm off-white background, near-black text, muted gray secondary text, extremely subtle borders, one accent colour. Clean sans-serif interface, optional display face, monospace for small technical labels. No gradients, neon glow, glassmorphism, large rounded cards, floating decoration, fake statistics or excessive animation. Light and dark both supported.
- Voice: human, confident, straightforward. No motivational filler.

## Product Principles

1. Every claim is checkable via a link.
2. The work leads; the chrome recedes.
3. Less, but better: every element must have a job.
